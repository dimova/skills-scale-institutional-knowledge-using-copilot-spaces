# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy and acceptance testing. They collaborate with Developers and Product Managers to define test coverage, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for each project
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Design and implement automated and manual test suites
- Track and report quality metrics and defect trends
- Identify and escalate quality risks during sprint planning and delivery
- Coordinate cross-environment testing (staging, production)

### Goals
- Ensure all features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Increase test coverage and automation rate
- Reduce time-to-quality in the delivery cycle

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning sessions to review and refine acceptance criteria with Developers and Product Managers
- Daily standups focused on quality blockers and test progress
- Quality reports and defect tracking shared with Project Managers
- Collaborative test-scenario definition with Developers

---

## Technical Architect/Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the technical approach, system design, and implementation strategy. They balance technical excellence with business needs while guiding Developers and collaborating with Product Managers and Project Managers to mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, design patterns, and component boundaries
- Review technical approaches and establish code quality standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and cross-team dependencies
- Guide technology decisions (tools, libraries, frameworks)
- Mentor Developers and lead technical design reviews
- Ensure scalability, security, and performance considerations are addressed

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, scalable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and unnecessary complexity
- Improve system reliability and long-term maintainability
- Build team capability through knowledge transfer and mentoring

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions with Developers
- Risk identification and mitigation planning with Project Managers
- Trade-off discussions with Product Managers on scope vs. technical constraints
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and technical documentation

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and decision authority. They ensure projects deliver value aligned with organizational goals and secure the necessary resources and approvals for successful execution.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and strategic context for projects
- Approve scope, timeline, and resource allocation
- Provide organizational and market insights to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Make escalated decisions and resolve conflicts between business priorities
- Monitor project health and validate business impact
- Communicate project value and outcomes to the broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure project alignment with strategic priorities
- Maximize business value delivered relative to investment
- Secure and protect project resources throughout delivery
- Reduce organizational risk and stakeholder friction

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff approvals and milestone gate reviews with Project Managers
- Monthly stakeholder updates and roadmap reviews with Product Managers
- Escalation forums for unresolved scope or resource conflicts
- Release announcements and business impact summaries

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and coach teams on effective agile practices. They work across all roles—Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers—to foster continuous improvement and team health.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove impediments that block team progress
- Coach the team on agile principles, values, and practices
- Track team velocity, cycle time, and delivery health metrics
- Shield the team from external distractions and unplanned interruptions
- Foster a culture of psychological safety and continuous improvement

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver predictably and sustainably
- Improve team collaboration, trust, and self-organization
- Reduce waste and friction in delivery processes
- Increase team satisfaction and engagement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies facilitated for Developers and Product Managers
- Impediment logs and escalations shared with Project Managers and Stakeholders
- Retrospective action items and improvement plans tracked transparently
- Coaching sessions and agile health check-ins with all roles

---

## Operations/DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and production systems. They enable Developers to deliver features reliably and securely to production while supporting Project Managers with release planning.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage cloud infrastructure, environments, and resource provisioning
- Implement security controls, compliance policies, and access management
- Monitor production systems and lead incident response and post-mortems
- Plan and execute deployments, rollbacks, and environment promotions
- Optimize infrastructure cost, performance, and reliability

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable deployments to production
- Maximize system uptime and service reliability
- Reduce deployment risk and minimize incident impact
- Maintain secure and cost-effective infrastructure

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment scheduling with Project Managers and Developers
- Incident response coordination and post-mortems shared across all roles
- Infrastructure performance and capacity reviews with Technical Architects
- Security and compliance audit updates for Stakeholders

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads own the user experience strategy and design system. They collaborate with Product Managers to validate customer needs and work closely with Developers to ensure usability and consistency are built into every feature.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX strategy, interaction patterns, and design system standards
- Conduct user research, usability testing, and accessibility reviews
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity design specifications
- Validate design decisions with users and synthesize feedback into improvements
- Collaborate with Developers on feasible, high-quality implementation of designs
- Maintain design documentation and ensure consistency across the product

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and customer-aligned user experiences
- Reduce usability issues and support tickets through proactive design validation
- Ensure the product design system scales across features and teams
- Champion the customer-first principle throughout the delivery lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and feature ideation sessions with Product Managers
- Design reviews and handoff sessions with Developers
- Usability test findings and design iteration updates shared with all roles
- Accessibility and design system standards documentation
